函数: dbCreateRect
签名: dbCreateRect(
d_cellView txl_layerPurposel_bBox
)
=> d_rect / nil
描述: Creates a rectangle.
来源: skdfref/chap2.html

参数:
  d_cellView — The cellview.
  txl_layerPurpose — Name, number, or a list of layer or layer-purpose pair.
    For more information, see Type txl_layerPurpose.
  l_bBox — Defines the lower left and upper right corners of the bounding box.

返回:
  d_rect — The dbObject of the rectangle.
  nil — The rectangle is not created.
